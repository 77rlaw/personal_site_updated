16-Bit Adder Architectures

Designed and implemented three 16-bit adder architectures in SystemVerilog: ripple-carry, carry-lookahead, and carry-select. Built a hierarchical 4×4 CLA using propagate/generate logic, wrote testbenches to verify the designs, and compared their critical-path timing in Vivado.
